## 🎯 Overview

**DRASTIC-Tox** is a web-based platform for assessing groundwater contamination risk in artisanal and industrial mining regions of Sudan. It extends the classical **DRASTIC** model (Aller et al., 1987) by integrating **cyanide (CN)** and **mercury (Hg)** toxicity indicators — the two primary contaminants in Sudanese artisanal gold mining.

**Impact:**
- 👥 **1M+** artisanal miners exposed
- 🚨 **4-5M** citizens at risk
- 💧 **Understudied** groundwater vulnerability in Sudan
